Role Overview

We are looking for a Remote Help Desk Technical to support our staff with day-to-day technical needs. This role involves assisting employees with system access, software usage, and general troubleshooting so that our internal teams can work efficiently.

Primary Responsibilities

Assist employees with technical questions through standard communication channels.
Identify and resolve common issues involving software, user accounts, and system access.
Record support requests and provide clear documentation.
Coordinate with senior IT staff when additional support is required.
Help maintain reliable access to company systems and digital tools.

Qualifications

High school diploma or equivalent required; additional education in IT is beneficial.
1–3 years of experience in a help desk or technical support role.
Comfortable working with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and general support utilities.
Strong communication and problem-solving skills.
Ability to work independently in a structured remote setting.
Access to a stable internet connection and a quiet workspace.
